# DoesItDiePlus — Retail

The test build contains **DoTInfo, ResourceDing, ShieldsInfo, SpellDamageInfo, CastPrediction and HealPrediction**, their settings and the shared look. It does not load Range, HunterRangeFinder or StatsInfo. The Mainline manifest covers Retail 12.1.0 (`120100`), 12.1.5 (`120105`) and 12.1.7 PTR (`120107`).

On 21 October 2026 the API sources of the live client [12.1.5.70448](https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/tree/b9be9379719047977fca4dba1bb1d9988e8840b3) and of the PTR [12.1.7.70424](https://github.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/tree/b87363e0770c2f15b1e2f154de8ad3b0cf3ea90a) were compared. Prediction drawing already skips secret frame-level values; GUIDs are keys for the current session only. Adding the PTR interface is based on reading the sources, not on a check in the game. 12.1.7 remains a PTR version according to [Blizzard's notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/midnight-echoes-of-the-worldcore-ptr-development-notes/2383391).

## Installation

1. Build the package: `python tools/build_zip.py --retail`.
2. Extract the `UsefulPlatesAndTooltips` folder from `dist/UsefulPlatesAndTooltips-<version>-retail.zip` (the version is the `## Version` line of `UsefulPlatesAndTooltips_Mainline.toc`) into `_retail_/Interface/AddOns/`. When replacing an older package, back up its folder and delete it first; do not extract the ZIP over the old files.
3. Enable the addon in the game. `/upt` opens the settings; `/reload` applies a module being switched on or off. Show enemy nameplates with the `V` key.

The Retail ZIP has its own `UsefulPlatesAndTooltips_Mainline.toc`; it contains neither the Forever manifest nor the other modules. A plain `python tools/build_zip.py` still builds the existing Forever package.

## Scope of the prototype

The DoTInfo estimated-damage bar recognises directly cast spells: Corruption, Wither, Agony, Siphon Life and Immolate (warlock), Shadow Word: Pain, Purge the Wicked, Holy Fire, Vampiric Touch and Devouring Plague (priest), Moonfire, Sunfire, Stellar Flare, Rake and Rip (druid), Garrote, Rupture, Kingsbane and Deathmark (rogue), Flame Shock (shaman), Living Bomb (mage), Serpent Sting (hunter), Fiery Brand (demon hunter) and Rend (warrior). The spells must be in the selected specialization and their description must be readable. The spells added in 0.9.96 (Immolate, Devouring Plague, Wither, Siphon Life, Purge the Wicked, Holy Fire, Stellar Flare, Living Bomb, Serpent Sting, Fiery Brand) come from a list of spell ids and descriptions from the database, without a check in the client; a spell whose description cannot be read simply shows nothing (`/dotinfo check` lists them). The parser covers English and German descriptions; it reads Spanish, French, Korean, Portuguese (BR) and Chinese descriptions only through a fallback path shared with Forever, not checked on such Retail clients. Unstable Affliction, Shadow Word: Madness, Barbed Shot, area effects, procs other than the rogue effects described below, channels and the additional recipients of Sunfire are outside this numeric model.

A refresh keeps the predicted remaining time up to 30% of the base DoT duration (Pandemic), for the effects that support it; the gold colour marks entering that window. Kingsbane, Deathmark and the automatic poisons get no extra Pandemic time. When the recipient of a cast is hidden, the addon assumes the selected target if there is no competing focus/mouseover, and skips ambiguous assignments. Whether this assumption holds for your own macros has to be checked in the game.

Since 0.9.55-retail every readable cast id has its own record of the recipient, the description and the combo points at the moment of sending. A second cast does not overwrite the first. Interrupts, failures, elapsed time and the loading screen remove the matching records; a late completion without a record is not assigned to a new target. With a hidden id, two pending casts of the same spell cannot be told apart and the estimate is skipped. The EN/DE parser skips optional talent blocks in square brackets.

The rogue also gets **icons of the bleeds and poisons that are really active**, with the native remaining time and stack count: Garrote, Rupture, Deadly Poison, Amplifying Poison, Kingsbane, Deathmark, Internal Bleeding and Mutilated Flesh/Doomblade. Amplifying Poison shows stacks, not a separate DoT. The icons of your own effects appear above the target bar and on enemy nameplates; they use the shared `showMarkers` and `nameplateMode` settings, and the icons above the target bar also follow "Show on target frame" (`targetMarkers`). Blizzard handles the aura state, timers and stacks. When the native aura container is unavailable, the icons stay off without blocking the other modules.

From **0.9.56-retail** the rogue's numeric bar also includes an experimental clock for **Deadly Poison / Tödliches Gift (2818)**. The periodic damage amount comes from the current Deadly Poison description (2823), without adding later instant hits. A readable `UNIT_AURA` identifies your own application/update of the poison on the target or nameplate. The clock counts the remaining damage down linearly; a recognised refresh replaces the previous estimate, without Pandemic. A readable expiration time synchronises the clock. With a hidden time but a readable effect id, instance and owner, the clock starts at the application or at an identified aura update, with the time from the description. A readable aura duration replaces the description's time when the client provides it.

This is a **prototype that needs checking in the game**: with a hidden expiration, an update of this particular aura is treated as a refresh, so changing other properties of the aura can inflate the time. General aura updates and rescans do not reset the clock. When Retail also hides the identity of the application or denies access to auras, the addon does not start a new estimate; an existing clock counts down the last recognised application to the end. `/dotinfo debug` shows the entries `POISON APPLY`, `POISON REFRESH`, `own clock`, `aura update hidden`, `aura list hidden` or `aura access denied`. They are also saved in the DoTInfo log. Merely applying the poison to a weapon, auto-attacks and damage from other players do not start this clock. A scan of an existing aura with a hidden time does not invent when it was applied.

From **0.9.58-retail** Deadly Poison also has a **native damage segment on the health bar** of the target and of enemy nameplates. Blizzard's native container picks its own aura 2818 and controls its visibility, countdown, refreshes and removal. The addon passes on the periodic amount from the description of 2823; the native binding displays an estimate proportional to the remaining time. It needs no readable `UNIT_AURA`, time or HP in Lua. In the DoTInfo settings enable `Show label` to also see the `~...` number above the right end of the bar. The bar and the number respect `showMarkers`, the nameplate switch-off and, on the target frame, `targetMarkers`. **0.9.61-retail** widens the marker from a 2-pixel line to the full HP height and keeps it above the bar even after a nameplate addon changes the frame level. It also fixes the conversion of the native time percentage: 100% of an amount of 66 means `~66`, instead of the wrong `~6600`.

From **0.9.62-retail** the native poison segment starts after the sum of the visible bleeds and other DoTs, instead of covering their start. Its position follows ticks, animation, expiration and target changes. In both clients the ordinary segments are grouped by the data available at the first display: known ticks, estimates from the description, an own clock and a cast in progress. The native poison stays last. An amount correction, a time synchronisation and a refresh of an active effect keep its place in the group, so refining an estimate does not shift the other segments. If a readable poison is already part of the ordinary sum, its native segment is zeroed so as not to double the damage. The poison uses the fill, DoT colour, transparency, outline and separator settings; on the target also the outline pulse, glow, shine and bar scrolling. Presets, including Juicy and Minimal, and the separate nameplate settings therefore cover the poison too. Changing the look replaces only the native poison segment and binds the real aura again, without recreating the bleed icons. On a protected target the change waits until the end of combat. One-off effects, such as the flash after a proc, are not triggered by reading the hidden visibility of the aura.

From **0.9.63-retail / 0.9.50 Forever** the `DoT colors` option has three modes: **By effect type**, **Rainbow** and a single fill colour. The default segment mode and Juicy use green for poisons/Nature damage, purple for curses/Shadow damage and red for bleeds/Physical damage; the other schools keep their own colours. The earlier `each` now uses these meaningful colours, and the former `school` mode is its alias. Rainbow gives every spell name its own colour and keeps it across refreshes, amount changes and reordering in that session. The mode is chosen separately in the Marker and Nameplates tabs; it also covers the native Deadly Poison segment. A single fill colour still respects the chosen colour.

The findings confirmed in Retail are shared with Forever: poison and bleed segments add up instead of overlapping; the look, type colours and Rainbow use the shared settings; an estimate correction keeps the order of the active effects. **0.9.51 Forever** adds recovery of missed aura events: with at least 0.25 s between scans of the same enemy it re-checks the readable auras of the target, softenemy and nameplates. One enemy is scanned once, even with several tokens. The synchronisation covers all five ranks of Deadly Poison, stacks, the refresh at five stacks, a late proc and removal of the effect. It keeps the learned/AP-adjusted bleed ticks. A scan with a hidden time does not refresh the own clock or guess when a new aura was applied; a refusal to read leaves the last recognised clock to run out. This scan works for a rogue with DoTInfo enabled. It needs its own confirmation in Forever after `/reload`; a Retail confirmation does not replace this test. The shared code is also in the **0.9.64-retail** package, where the synchronisation of hidden auras is still driven by the native containers.

This native estimate works separately from the DoTInfo damage sum and skull; the hidden amount cannot be added to them in Lua. It does not account for additional instant hits or for changes to the damage per tick by talents and other modifiers. With readable dimensions of the whole bar, the segment is moved on the full HP scale and clipped at the right edge only after the countdown. When the dimensions of the whole bar are hidden, or the full poison amount alone exceeds the enemy's maximum HP, the fallback variant may clip the width before the countdown and understate the share of damage; the number keeps the estimate from the description. It needs the native aura container API and time formatting. The log records `NATIVE POISON damage display ready`, `duration formatting unavailable`, `setup failed` or `update failed`. The rendering of hidden values still has to be confirmed in the game.

**0.9.59-retail** completes the refresh after a report that the bar appeared only on a proc from the first hit. The `UNIT_AURA` event requests a full native scan of that token's container, without reading the event's contents. The shared refresher additionally orders a scan of the active target and nameplate no more often than every 0.25 s. This periodic scan also works with a hidden token or missing updates. Every scan binds the current aura and its real expiration; it does not start its own clock again. The mock checks a late proc, a refresh, expiration, removal and reapplication on the same nameplate with the incremental updates missed. The fix has to be checked in the game with a first hit without a proc and a poison applied only later.

**0.9.60-retail** fixes a display-creation error confirmed in the client log: `DurationTextBinding:SetTextFormat(): expected 0 format components for 1 placeholders`. That function needs `{}` placeholders, not `%s`; formatting the amount itself through `NumericRuleFormatter` still uses `~%.0f`. The earlier error interrupted the text setup after the time bar was created, so the partially created bar was not scaled to the damage and HP. The change covers the refresh from 0.9.59; how the whole thing works after `/reload` has to be confirmed in the game.

From **0.9.57-retail / 0.9.47 Forever** the shared module `RogueEffects.lua` synchronises your own Garrote and Rupture with the readable aura: expiration time, refresh and removal. A readable aura tooltip with the current damage per tick takes precedence over the base description. A new Rupture without a record of the combo points spent needs such a current amount; the addon does not guess the maximum points. In Forever the Garrote/Rupture ranks and the learned tick sizes are kept, including AP-dependent damage. Deadly Poison exists in the Forever data in five ranks: the module recognises their separate ids, reads the current description of the given rank and counts the readable stacks up to five. A refresh at five stacks also corrects the clock. The current amount from the aura tooltip is not multiplied by the stacks again.

In Retail the current aura tooltip can also feed **Kingsbane, Internal Bleeding and Mutilated Flesh/Doomblade**. Empty descriptions and a talent percentage are not treated as a DoT amount. An update of Kingsbane's damage does not restart its clock when the expiration is hidden. Without a readable aura amount, the estimate from your own Kingsbane cast remains, if the periodic part of its description can be read; the additional bleeds from talents are skipped then. **Deathmark** adds its own bleed from the description or the aura tooltip, without counting its percentage modifier as damage. Switching targets keeps the clocks separate for each enemy; the loading screen clears them in both clients. The Forever rank ids were checked in the client data, and the scope of poisons and bleeds against [Blizzard's rogue description](https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/24310968/world-of-warcraft-forever-class-deep-dives-rogue-and-warlock).

Amplifying, Instant and Wound Poison add no separate periodic DoT, and Crippling/Numbing deal no periodic damage. In the current Retail Crimson Tempest copies the existing Garrote/Rupture: the native container may show these copies, but the addon does not add a separate estimated Crimson Tempest DoT.

The DoT overlay remains a child of the whole nameplate frame. Its size is fitted separately to the real health bar and converted between their scales. When the dimensions or the scale are hidden, the native anchors stay without guessing a size. Since 0.9.50-retail the shared bar choice also recognises Platynator's active health widget; that addon hides Blizzard's original bar. DoTInfo, the rogue icons, ResourceDing and ShieldsInfo use the visible bar instead of the hidden frame, also after a style or size change. The same bar choice and scale conversion are used in the Forever package; the look in that client has to be checked in the game. The integration matches the local Platynator version `499-1-g4e9cab2`; its internal widget layout may change in the future.

`/dotinfo plates` records the positions and scales of the frame, the chosen bar, the marker and the segment, and the current game settings. Run it with a visible enemy carrying a DoT, then `/reload` to save the measurement in the DoTInfo log. The API may refuse to read the positions of nameplate regions; the log then keeps the information about the refusal instead of doing arithmetic on that data.

ResourceDing covers the rogue/druid combo points, the warlock's Soul Shards, the paladin's Holy Power, the Windwalker monk's Chi, the Arcane mage's Arcane Charges and the evoker's Essence. Visibility and sounds depend on the active resource, form and specialization.

ShieldsInfo shows the remaining absorb on the available player, target, group and nameplate frames. Secret HP and absorb values are passed directly to the native bars; the addon neither reads them as numbers nor supplies its own text values. With an active DoT marker the shield takes the narrow top edge of the bar. The availability of the frame and the client's consent to extend it may limit the display.

SpellDamageInfo adds damage/healing numbers to Blizzard's native action bars, to EllesmereUI 9.5.5 buttons, and lines to tooltips, based on the available spell description. Bartender4 and ElvUI buttons (LibActionButton) get the numbers through the library; this path has not been checked in the game yet. Retail uses the amounts from the description without adding SP/AP by the old formulas, and finishers take into account the current combo point limit returned by the API. These are estimates with no guarantee of the real hit, crit, target resistance or the full effect of talents. A description or action marked as secret may make it impossible to show the number. Accuracy for each class and specialization has to be checked in the game.

CastPrediction shows the predicted damage of the spell being cast right now (without DoTs) as a marker on the target's health bar and its nameplate — the same idea as the DoTInfo marker. The damage range comes from the spell description; on Forever the spell power is added through a coefficient, together with the detected passive talents (the client's talent tree) and readable auras (Arcane Power, Improved Scorch…), while on Retail the description already contains all these components, so nothing is added. Critical hits, procs and partial resists are not predicted, and the "will kill" icon compares the lowest end of the range. On Retail the module learns from hits only for direct physical damage (the spell description holds your numbers but not the target's armor: in a saved log, Zuverlässiger Schuss described as 86 hit for 60–61, about 70%; magic is not reduced by armor); on Forever consecutive non-crits calibrate the estimate, and each talent/aura can be forced to Auto / Off / Max. Settings are in `/upt cast`; `/upt castpred talents` and `/upt castpred auras` list what the client answers.

Damage channels (e.g. Arcane Missiles, Mind Flay) use the same markers on the selected enemy's HP and its nameplate. The estimate shrinks in proportion to the remaining channel time; an interrupt, the end and a target change remove the marker. Channel ticks do not calibrate the full hit. Readable client times and a recognised damage description are needed; the module does not determine AoE recipients or the charge level of empowered spells.

With action targeting, casts and DoTs may use `softenemy` when no ordinary hostile target is selected, also while a friendly pet is selected. A dead target is not a recipient (after its death the cast goes to `softenemy`), and a living ordinary target takes precedence over `softenemy`, also with the same name (a pack of identical mobs). The marker stays on the nameplate of the original recipient. The numbers on Forever's native gamepad crossbar are handled by SpellDamageInfo; this integration works only where the client provides that interface.

Look of the prediction: a solid translucent surface for a direct hit, vertical stripes for channeling, school colours consistent with DoTInfo by default. The overlay retreats from the current HP edge and is clipped to the bar: 20 damage at 60/100 HP covers the range 60–40. The scale stays based on the maximum HP, and the anchor follows the native health texture.

HealPrediction draws incoming healing on player, target, focus, Blizzard's target-of-target, party and raid frames, starting at the current health edge. Direct heals use the client's `UnitGetIncomingHeals` projection, with hidden native values passed unchanged to the bars. The "All healers" option uses the client's total instead of only your own heals. Supported targeted HoTs use separate estimates from readable descriptions; readable own buffs recover their remaining time and removal, while blocked aura access retains the last known cast estimate. This does not provide complete coverage of channels, pet heals, triggered or stacking/expiry-based effects. `/upt healprobe` lists the native readings; `/upt healdebug [player|target|focus|targettarget]` also reports HoT and overlay state with hidden values labelled `SECRET`. Settings are in `/upt heals`. The new focus/target-of-target paths and this Retail package have not been checked in game.

The EllesmereUI 9.5.5 integration picks its visible nameplate bars and the active player/target frames, including after a bar is replaced or the frame source changes in the profile. DoTInfo, the native rogue auras, ResourceDing and ShieldsInfo use these hosts. Shields also handle EUI group frames. The `EABButton1..180` buttons are detected without writing to their protected attributes; the current slot comes from the `action` attribute, also after a page change. The Forever package uses the same detection and the anchors for Range/Hunter, and the public API of the upgrade arrows in EUI bags. Rendering, the order of events and frame protection still have to be checked in the game. The EUI modules can show their own auras, resources and absorb in parallel — their visibility is set separately in EUI.

Since 0.9.85-retail (Forever 0.9.72) shields also cover EUI's separate party view and follow the orientation and the reversed HP fill when the profile changes. In Forever the "Grey out action bar icons" option also works on EUI buttons: it keeps its colours and desaturation, and restores them after a range change, loss of the target or switching the option off. It does not change EUI's range-check subscription. The Range module remains available only in the Forever package.

The refresh model and the spell ids were checked against [SimulationCraft data for Midnight](https://github.com/simulationcraft/simc/blob/midnight/engine/dbc/generated/sc_spell_data.inc). The loading screen clears the predicted DoT damage; a readable tooltip of the active rogue aura and its time can recreate it. A hidden aura gives no such possibility. After the player leaves combat the rogue predictions are cleared, also when aura reading is blocked, if the visible NPC has also left combat. Effects read in a partial scan remain; players and NPCs still in combat keep their predictions. The native rogue icons show the current effects independently of this estimate.

Healthstone, Crimson Vial (Blutrote Phiole) and Recuperate (Gesundung) show on the action bar the amount of HP that corresponds to the healing percentage in the description (EN/DE). For Crimson Vial this is the whole healing over 4 seconds, and for Recuperate over 10 seconds, not a single tick. The number updates when the maximum health changes. When the game hides the maximum HP, the button shows the percentage, e.g. `20% HP`, instead of an outdated number.

## Additional percentage healing (0.9.54-retail)

The EN/DE parser also handles Renewal, Healing Elixir, Death Pact, Bitter Immunity, Exhilaration, Victory Rush / Impending Victory, Regeneratin’ and Cannibalize. The percentage comes from the current description in the game, without a table of fixed values for the spells.

Cannibalize adds up the given percentage per tick over the given time. Exhilaration counts only the player; additional healing over time is added if it appears in the active description and not in the optional talent block. Death Pact shows the full immediate healing amount, without subtracting the later healing absorption. For attacks with healing, the readable damage stays on the button and the healing is a separate green number. With a hidden maximum HP the healing takes the form of a percentage.

The descriptions were checked in the Retail client data: [Renewal](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=108238), [Exhilaration](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=109304), [Victory Rush](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=34428), [Impending Victory](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=202168), [Healing Elixir](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=122281), [Death Pact](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=48743), [Bitter Immunity](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=383762), [Regeneratin’](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=291944), [Cannibalize](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=20577).

Desperate Prayer stays outside this model: it increases the maximum HP and heals by the related amount at the same time. Healing that depends on damage taken, missing health, the target's HP or the pet's HP also needs a separate model. It is not replaced by a percentage of the player's health.

## What to check in the game

- **ResourceDing:** the sound on reaching the resource maximum, gaining it again after spending, a specialization change, druid forms and the target. Resources depend on the class and specialization; mana or a resource may be unavailable to the code in combat.
- **DoTInfo:** apply a DoT, refresh it, switch targets and repeat on two enemies. Also check casting on the soft target and mouseover. With a hidden recipient the addon should skip an uncertain assignment.
- **Rogue icons:** apply Garrote/Rupture and a poison, also through auto-attack; check the time, stacks and expiration on the target and on several nameplates. Check that the icons hide through `showMarkers` and `nameplateMode`, and that "Show on target frame" off removes only the target bar's icons.
- **Deadly Poison clock:** after `/reload` enable `/dotinfo debug`, apply the poison to a weapon and hit an enemy until the first proc. Check `POISON APPLY` and the decreasing estimate; later procs should show `POISON REFRESH`. Switch targets, poison a second enemy, go back to the first; the clocks must stay separate. Stop attacking and check the expiration. Repeat in a context with restricted access to auras. An entry `aura access denied`/`aura list hidden` means there is no application signal; an offline test does not prove that it works in that context.
- **Native Deadly Poison bar:** with `aura access denied`, check the full-HP-height segment, in the chosen style, behind the bleed segments and, with `Show label` on, the decreasing `~...` number. They should appear only after your own proc, refresh with the aura and disappear on expiration/removal. Be sure to check the first hit without a proc, a later proc, a refresh and reapplication after expiration without changing the target. Repeat on two enemies, on a target change and on nameplate reuse, and with Platynator. Check `/reload`, an amount change in the description and the display settings. Right after application the number should match the periodic amount from the description, not a hundred times that; the mock does not confirm rendering in the game engine.
- **Other rogue effects:** apply Garrote/Rupture and remove them or wait until they end; the estimate should vanish together with the effect. In Forever check the growth of Deadly Poison stacks and the refresh at five stacks. In Retail check the growth of Kingsbane's damage without extending its time, Deathmark's own bleed and the availability of the current amount for Internal Bleeding/Doomblade. When the aura amount is unavailable these procs should be skipped, without a Lua error and without an amount from a talent percentage.
- **ShieldsInfo:** a shield on the player and the target, absorb running out, group/raid, and a shield plus DoT on a shared nameplate. Check with hidden HP in combat.
- **SpellDamageInfo:** ordinary spells and finishers, the tooltip after changing talents/resource, macros and an action bar page change, entering/leaving combat. Rupture and Rip also handle lines without the word "damage"/"Schaden" if the description speaks of dealing damage. For the reported Rupture description `3 Punkte: 442 im Verlauf von 16 Sek.` the action bar should show 442 at three combo points. Hidden data should omit the number, without Lua errors.
- **Look:** consistency of the marker on the target frame and the nameplate, several enemies, entering/leaving combat, `/reload`, no Lua errors.

The DoTInfo bar and skull predict damage from your own cast of the spell, a readable application of a rogue effect and the available description of the spell or aura; they do not read damage from hidden auras or real ticks. Talents, refreshes, haste, damage modifiers and the limits of secret API values may change the result. The skull is an estimate of the remaining damage relative to HP. The native rogue icons work independently of this model. Version 0.9.47-retail made the nameplate overlay disappear after the frame's parent changed; 0.9.48-retail restores the earlier parent and fits the geometry separately. The position and height of the 0.9.50-retail overlay were confirmed in the game with Platynator. How the other classes and modules work, and the look in Forever, have to be checked in the game.
